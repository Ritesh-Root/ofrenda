OFRENDA CLI ALTAR: art, colour map, animation
=================================================

Rendered by `ofrenda altar [--person rosa-mendez] [--still]`. 64 columns wide, fits an 80x24 terminal.
Colour by role (see COLOUR MAP). Letters in the art are role codes, NOT printed:
the renderer replaces each glyph with itself and paints it with the role of the matching
cell in the MASK below. Keep the art and the mask the same width, line for line.

MASK (same geometry; one code per cell)
---------------------------------------
P = papel picado, cycles R/F/T per flag   N = name card text     C = candle wax
L = flame (animated)                       M = marigold @          I = item text
S = altar shelf =                          W = petal path ~        H = hint/footer text
. = no colour (terminal default)

Fallback: NO_COLOR=1 or a non-TTY prints the art uncoloured. --still disables animation.

ANIMATION
---------
Flame frames, 180 ms each, random phase per candle (never in sync):
  frame 0:  (^)     frame 1:  (')     frame 2:  (^)     frame 3:  (`)
  frame 1 and 3 dim the flame colour one step (ansi 208 / 172).
Petal path: every 600 ms shift the ~ row one cell to the left (a slow drift toward the altar).
Papel picado: every 900 ms swap \/ and /\ on the scalloped top edge (a breath of wind).
Stop all animation after 12 s or on any key, then leave the still frame on screen.
Respect reduced motion: OFRENDA_STILL=1 or --still.

ITEM LINES
----------
The two item rows are generated: up to 3 food items on the top shelf, then up to 3 of
song/place/ritual on the lower shelf, from the person's ofrenda_items (most-cited first).
Truncate each to 16 chars with an ellipsis. Centre each row between the candles.
